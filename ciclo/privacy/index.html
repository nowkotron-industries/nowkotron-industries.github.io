Ciclo privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short: Ciclo doesn’t collect any data. Everything you log is stored on your phone and, if Google backup is on, in that backup, encrypted with your screen lock. Nobody else, including whoever made the app, has access to it.

Who publishes Ciclo

Ciclo is an app by Nowkotron Industries. For any question about this policy or your data, write to nowkotron.industries@gmail.com.

What the app stores

To work, Ciclo stores on your phone:

  • The start and end dates of the periods you log.
  • Your settings: usual cycle and period length, your sidekick’s look and which notifications you want.
  • Your sidekick drawing, if you make one.
  • Today’s answer to “How are you?”. It’s replaced the next day and no history is kept.
  • The last message you wrote for “Make an announcement”, if you write one.

Where it’s stored

On your phone. Ciclo has no accounts or servers of its own, shows no ads and includes no third-party services or analytics tools. The app has no permission to connect to the internet, so it can’t send anything anywhere.

Google backup

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android includes Ciclo’s data in it, so it comes back by itself if you switch to another phone with the same Google account or reinstall the app. Ciclo only allows this backup when it’s end-to-end encrypted with your screen lock PIN, pattern or password: neither Google nor whoever made the app can read it. If your phone has no screen lock, or runs Android 8, Ciclo’s data isn’t backed up.

This backup is an Android service between you and Google: the app sends nothing on its own and receives nothing from it. You can turn it off in Android’s settings, under backup. When you move your data to a new phone by cable or wirelessly, Ciclo’s data moves too.

What leaves the app because you decide so

Only what you choose to share or save leaves Ciclo:

  • Make an announcement: a picture with a phrase that you send with the app you choose. It only reaches whoever you decide.
  • Report: a PDF with your period dates that you can share, save or print.
  • Backups: a file with the app’s data that you save wherever you choose. This file has no password: keep it somewhere you trust.

From then on, those files are under your control and under the terms of the app or service you use to send or store them.

Permissions

The only permission Ciclo asks for is to show notifications, which let you know when your period is coming soon or ask whether it has started or ended. It’s optional: you can decline it or turn each reminder off in Settings.

Android also grants the app a few technical permissions to schedule those reminders, for example to schedule them again after you restart your phone. None of them gives access to the internet or to your data.

How to delete your data

Go to Settings › Your data › “Delete all my data”. Uninstalling the app also deletes everything from the phone. The Google backup is emptied the next time it updates after you delete your data; if you uninstall the app, it’s kept in case you install it again, and you can delete it from your Google account’s backup. Backup files you saved yourself aren’t deleted: you can delete them yourself.

Children

Ciclo doesn’t collect data from anyone, children included.

Health

Ciclo’s dates are estimates based on what you log. The app is not a contraceptive method, can’t be used to plan a pregnancy and doesn’t replace medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, it will be published here with its new date.
